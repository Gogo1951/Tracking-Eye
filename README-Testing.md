# Tracking Eye // Manual Test Plan

This is the manual test plan for Tracking Eye, the steps to confirm it works before a release is tagged. For what it does, see [README.md](https://github.com/Gogo1951/Tracking-Eye/blob/main/README.md); for how it works, see [README-Technical.md](https://github.com/Gogo1951/Tracking-Eye/blob/main/README-Technical.md).

## Before you start

**Run the whole list on each flavor in turn: Classic Era, Season of Discovery on the Classic Era client, TBC Anniversary, WoW Forever, MoP Classic, and Retail.** Steps are numbered continuously so you can report "failed on step N."

Gather these once so you aren't caught short mid-run:

- **A gatherer**: a character with **Herbalism and Mining**, on a class with no tracking of its own. It carries most of the plan, since Find Herbs and Find Minerals are the two abilities Farm Mode exists to rotate. A Shaman is ideal, because it also covers Ghost Wolf.
- **A Hunter** that knows Track Beasts, Track Undead, Track Humanoids, and at least two other tracking abilities, plus Aspect of the Cheetah and Aspect of the Pack.
- **A Druid with Herbalism**, with Cat Form, Travel Form, and Cat Form's own Track Humanoids.
- **A Shaman with Ghost Wolf and Herbalism or Mining**, if your gatherer isn't one.
- **An angler** that knows Find Fish and one other tracking ability, with a fishing pole in its bags, on every flavor but Classic Era, which has no Find Fish.
- **A Come & Get It character** with neither Herbalism nor Mining that isn't a Rogue. A fresh low-level character is ideal.
- **A character that ran the previous release**, on every flavor but MoP Classic and Retail, which had none.
- **In your bags:** a locked lockbox, such as a Battered or Worn Lockbox. It is a negative test.
- **Out in the world:** herb nodes and ore veins, a locked treasure chest (Battered and Tattered chests along the Wetlands and Hillsbrad coastlines are reliable on the Classic clients), hostile beasts and undead, a critter, a town or inn, a dungeon entrance, a flight path, something to fight, and a mount for the gatherer and the Hunter.
- **Lua errors on screen:** type `/console scriptErrors 1` once, or run BugSack, or a broken step can pass in silence.
- **A non-English client**, only for the optional last step.

No second player is needed: Tracking Eye never sends chat on its own. Unless a step says otherwise, be out of combat and out in the open world. "Trigger a draft" always means: on the Come & Get It character, right-click an herb, vein, or locked chest you can't gather, and wait more than five seconds between attempts. Tracking only comes back on its own while you're moving, so keep walking wherever a step waits for it. Running from an unpackaged working copy, the version reads **Dev**, which is correct.

This plan deliberately skips the long tail: it doesn't re-walk every Farm Mode pause reason or every creature type a hunt can resolve, and it leaves out Hunter: Track Humanoids in Battlegrounds (it needs a battleground), resurrections that land mid-fight (they need a second player), the Profiles page, and most Diagnostic Tools reports.

## Verify this release's changes

This release gives each flavor its own TOC and adds MoP Classic and Retail, makes every automatic cast wait until you're clear and on the move, turns Automatic Target Tracking into a temporary hunt that never replaces your own pick, shows every option on every class, adds Zoom Mini-map Out and three Persistent Tracking options, and builds Come & Get It in. Run these fourteen first, on every flavor.

**One TOC per flavor**

**1.** At character select, open the **AddOns** list. Tracking Eye must be listed once, with its icon beside the name, and must not be marked out of date. **MoP Classic and Retail are new in this release, so this is the first time the add-on shows up there at all.** Then log in on a character that ran the previous release: its Persistent Tracking Ability, Farm Mode toggles and ticked abilities, Cycle Speed, and mini-map button position must all be exactly as you left them. A Persistent Tracking Ability that the previous release's Automatic Target Tracking picked from a target stays until you pick again, which is expected. Failure is the add-on missing or out of date, a blank square where its icon belongs, or any of those settings back at install defaults.

**2.** Open `/te`, go to **Diagnostic Tools**, tick **Enable Diagnostic Tools**, and click **Check Player & Tracking Spells**. The report's first line must name this client correctly: `Flavor Vanilla // Data Vanilla` on Classic Era, `Flavor Vanilla // Data Discovery` on Season of Discovery, `Flavor TBC // Data TBC` on TBC Anniversary, `Flavor Camelot // Data Camelot` on WoW Forever, `Flavor Mists // Data Mists` on MoP Classic, and `Flavor Mainline // Data Mainline` on Retail. Now click **Validate Vanilla/Spells-Vanilla.lua** and **Validate Vanilla/Zones-Vanilla.lua** (each flavor's buttons carry its own data folder in place of Vanilla) and wait until each progress line gives way to a finished report. The Spells report's tally line must read `0 NOT ON CLIENT` and `0 ERROR`, and neither report may hold a `TABLE MISSING` row; an `INCOMPLETE` count is not a failure. **Season of Discovery, MoP Classic, and Retail run data copied from another flavor that has never been checked on their own client, so this step is most likely to fail there.** Failure is a wrong flavor or data name, or any of those rows: paste the whole report into your bug report, since it names the exact entries to fix.

**Every option on every class**

**3.** On the gatherer, open `/te`. The main page must show **Automatic Target Tracking** as its own section, and three indented options under **Enable Persistent Tracking**: **Find Fish when you Equip a Fishing Pole**, **Druid: Track Humanoids when you Shift into Cat Form**, and **Hunter: Track Humanoids in Battlegrounds**. On the **Farm Mode** page, **Farm Mode Conditions** must offer **Mounted** and **Not Mounted**, then after a gap **Druid: Travel Forms**, **Hunter: Aspect of the Cheetah**, **Hunter: Aspect of the Pack**, and **Shaman: Ghost Wolf**. **Farm Mode Abilities** must open with **Include Persistent Tracking Ability**, then list every tracking ability this client has, learned or not, in bordered groups: **Professions & Racial Abilities** first, then **Druid**, **Hunter**, **Warlock**, and **Paladin**, each class heading in its class color. Hover an ability: its own in-game spell tooltip must show. Untick **Enable Farm Mode**: everything below it must vanish. Tick it again. **Each flavor shows its own set here:** Classic Era has no Find Fish anywhere; MoP Classic has no Warlock or Paladin group; Retail has no Hunter rows under Farm Mode Conditions and no Warlock group, and adds Track Beasts under Druid and Track Mechanicals under Hunter; MoP Classic and Retail list Track Pets with the professions. Only Classic Era, Season of Discovery included, offers **Use the Default Tracking Button** on the main page: tick it there and click Blizzard's own tracking icon on the mini-map, and only the Tracking Eye menu may open; untick it and Blizzard's icon must behave as before. Failure is a section, row, or group missing, one a flavor shouldn't have, a hover that shows only the name, or two menus at once.

**Automatic casts wait until you're clear**

**4.** On the gatherer, pick **Find Herbs** from the Tracking Menu and ride through the open world with Farm Mode cycling. Let a mob attack you and stay mounted: hover the button, and **Farm Mode Status** must read **Paused** with *"In combat."* underneath; no tracking cast may go off until the fight ends. Let another attack you, dismount mid-fight, and finish it on foot: nothing may be cast during the fight, and once it's over and you walk on, Find Herbs must come back within a tick or two. Mount up, let the cycle move on to Find Minerals, Shift + Left-Click the button to switch Farm Mode off while riding, and move the mouse away: Find Herbs must come back within a tick or two. Failure is *"Not mounted."* while you're on your mount, a tracking cast mid-fight, or the cycle's last ability left up after Farm Mode goes off.

**Standing still, a target, or a tooltip holds Farm Mode**

**5.** Still on the gatherer with Farm Mode on, stop your mount in the open world and hover the button: the status must read **Paused** with *"Standing still."*, and the cycle must stop. Turn on autorun (Num Lock) and it must resume within a tick. Riding on, select a hostile mob without attacking it: *"Targeting something you can attack."*, and no casts until you clear your target; selecting a friendly NPC or a corpse must not pause it. With autorun still on, hover an item in your bags, then the Tracking Eye button itself: each time the button's icon must stop changing until you move the mouse away, and over the button, with nothing else holding the cycle, the status must read *"Reading a tooltip."* Failure is a cast while you stand still, target something you can attack, or read a tooltip, or the cycle not resuming once you move on.

**Zoom Mini-map Out**

**6.** On the gatherer, zoom the mini-map all the way in, then mount up and ride with Farm Mode on. As the cycle's first cast goes out, the mini-map must zoom all the way out, with its zoom-in button usable and its zoom-out button greyed. Zoom back in while riding: it must stay where you put it for the rest of the run. Dismount, walk a few steps, and mount again: the new run must zoom it out again. Untick **Zoom Mini-map Out** under **Enable Farm Mode** and repeat: the zoom must never change. **WoW Forever and Retail keep these zoom buttons in a different place, so watch them there.** Failure is no zoom, a zoom-in button left greyed so you can't zoom back in, a zoom that fights you mid-run, or a Lua error.

**Automatic Target Tracking is a temporary hunt**

**7.** On the Hunter, pick **Track Humanoids** from the Tracking Menu, switch Farm Mode off (Shift + Left-Click the button) and Automatic Target Tracking on (Shift + Right-Click), and stay on foot. Target a hostile beast out in the world: tracking must switch to **Track Beasts** within a second or two, while the tooltip's **Persistent Tracking Ability** still reads **Track Humanoids**. Target a hostile undead: **Track Undead**. Kill it, loot it, and clear your target: the hunt must stay. Target a friendly NPC, a corpse, a critter, and an enemy player if one is around: nothing may change. Let a mob of a new kind attack you and target it only once the fight has started: nothing may change, during the fight or after it. **On Retail a mechanical switches to Track Mechanicals; everywhere else a mechanical must change nothing.** Failure is no switch, the Persistent Tracking Ability row changing, or a switch from anything but a living enemy creature targeted out of combat.

**8.** Set up as in step 7 and start a hunt before each of these, walking on after each: walk into a town or inn (Track Humanoids must come back within a few seconds of your portrait showing you're resting); zone into a dungeon (it must come back about ten seconds after the loading screen, and targeting creatures inside must do nothing); take a flight path (it must be up once you land and walk on); `/reload` (it must come back about ten seconds later); Shift + Right-Click to switch the feature off (it must come back), then on again; pick an ability from the Tracking Menu (your pick replaces the hunt and becomes your Persistent Tracking Ability); and Right-Click to Clear Tracking (tracking clears and stays clear). Failure is a hunt surviving any of these, or your own pick not coming back where it should.

**9.** Pick Track Humanoids again, tick two of the Hunter's other tracking abilities under **Farm Mode Abilities** (Track Hidden and Track Elementals, say), make sure **Include Persistent Tracking Ability** is ticked, switch Farm Mode back on, start a hunt on Track Beasts, and ride: Track Beasts must rotate with the other two, in Track Humanoids' place. Dismount and walk: Track Beasts must be what you're left tracking. Untick **Include Persistent Tracking Ability**, mount up, and target an undead while the cycle runs: no Track Undead cast may go off while you ride, and it must come back once you dismount and walk. Last, untick **Enable Persistent Tracking**: targeting a beast must still switch to Track Beasts, but dismounting, dying, or cancelling tracking from Blizzard's own tracking button must never recast anything. Tick it again when you're done. Failure is Track Humanoids in the rotation during a hunt, a hunt cast mid-cycle with the toggle unticked, the hunt not coming back after you dismount, or any recast with Persistent Tracking off.

**New Persistent Tracking options**

**10.** On the angler, with **Find Fish when you Equip a Fishing Pole** ticked (it is by default), pick your other tracking ability, equip a fishing pole, and walk: **Find Fish** must come up within a couple of seconds. Put the pole away and walk: your own pick must come back. On the Druid, tick **Druid: Track Humanoids when you Shift into Cat Form**, pick Find Herbs, then shift into Cat Form and walk: Track Humanoids must come up. Shift out and walk: Find Herbs must come back. Shift into Cat Form and Prowl at once: nothing may be cast until you leave Prowl. Untick each option and repeat: nothing may change. **Classic Era has no Find Fish, so the angler half doesn't run there.** Failure is Find Fish or Track Humanoids never coming up, your own pick not coming back, a cast while you Prowl, or anything happening with the option unticked.

**Come & Get It is built in**

**11.** Open `/te`: **Come & Get It** must sit directly beneath **Farm Mode** in the category list. Its page must show a description, **Enable Come & Get It** ticked, **Default Output** with its dropdown beside it on one line, and under that a silver line reading *"Note: Local (/1) only reaches players on your layer."* Hovering the dropdown must show a tip about Guild rather than repeat the note, and the page must carry no /Commands, welcome-message, or Feedback & Support rows. On a character that ran the previous release it starts at these defaults, even if the standalone Come & Get It was set up differently: nothing carries over, which is expected. Open the dropdown: exactly **Local (/1)**, **Say**, **Yell**, **Party**, **Guild**, in that order. Pick **Say** and trigger a draft: the chat box must open already switched to Say, holding the message alone. Set it back to **Local (/1)** and trigger one: the box must open switched to General. Untick the toggle: Default Output and its note must vanish. Tick it again. Failure is the page missing or out of order, the toggle off on a new character, a raw key such as `OPTIONS_OUTPUT_SAY`, the wrong channel, or `/say` sitting inside the message as words.

**12.** On the Come & Get It character, right-click an herb node. Your chat box must open holding a draft in exactly this shape: `Hey Herbalists! Peacebloom at 42, 68 in Elwynn Forest.` Then right-click an ore vein (`Hey Miners!` and the vein's name) and a locked world chest (`Hey Rogues!` and the chest's name). The coordinates must be whole numbers that match your world map within a point or two, and the place must be the zone, not a subzone. **This step is flavor-sensitive: each client words and numbers its errors differently, and Come & Get It has never run on MoP Classic or Retail, so a pass on one flavor proves nothing about another.** Where a flavor has no locked chest you can reach, say so in your report rather than failing the step. Failure is nothing happening, a vein addressed to Herbalists, a draft that sends itself, or `nil` anywhere in the line.

**13.** Each of these must produce **no draft**: right-clicking a node mid-fight (and nothing may appear once the fight ends); right-clicking one inside a dungeon; a second right-click within five seconds of a draft; a right-click while half-typed text sits in your chat box (your text must be left exactly as it was); right-clicking the locked lockbox in your bags; and any node with **Enable Come & Get It** unticked. Failure is a draft in any of these cases.

**Combat on WoW Forever and Retail**

**14.** On WoW Forever and Retail, where combat hides much of what add-ons can read, fight several mobs back to back for half a minute on each of these: the gatherer with **Not Mounted** ticked and Farm Mode on, hovering the button now and then (the status must read **Paused** with *"In combat."*); the Hunter with Automatic Target Tracking on, targeting new kinds mid-fight (nothing may switch); and the Druid, shifting forms mid-fight. No Lua error may appear during or after any fight, and once each fight ends and you walk on, the cycle must resume and your tracking must come back. **These two clients are the ones that break here: the others never hide those reads, so a pass on them proves nothing.** Failure is any Lua error, above all one about a secret value, a tooltip that fails to draw, or Farm Mode stuck after the fight.

When steps 1-14 pass on every flavor, this release's changes are verified. Proceed to `4 - Pre-Launch Review Prompt.md`.

## Core checks

**15.** Log in with Tracking Eye enabled. No Lua error may appear, and one line must print in chat: *"Tracking Eye // Version Dev. Settings (including the option to disable this message) can be found under Options > AddOns > Tracking Eye. Enjoying the add-on? Tell a friend about it! (="*, with the release number in place of Dev on a packaged build. Type `/reload`: the UI must come back clean and the same line must print again. Failure is any error naming Tracking Eye, or a welcome line with `nil` or a stray `%s` in it.

**16.** Open the settings three ways in turn: type `/te`, the add-on's only slash command; Shift + Middle-Click the mini-map button; and press `Esc`, choose **Options**, then **AddOns**, and select **Tracking Eye**. Each must show the settings **docked inside the Blizzard Options window**, with Tracking Eye selected in the category list on the left and **Farm Mode**, **Come & Get It**, **Profiles**, and **Diagnostic Tools** beneath it in that order. Failure looks like either nothing happening at all, or a standalone window floating free of the Options frame. **TBC Anniversary is the flavor that historically breaks this, so a tester who runs only Classic Era has not finished.**

**17.** Pull a mob and, while still in combat, type `/te`, then Shift + Middle-Click the mini-map button. Both times chat must print *"Tracking Eye // As a safety precaution, the Options Interface cannot be opened during combat."* and the panel must stay shut. Finish the fight and stand still: the panel must **not** open by itself afterwards. Failure is the panel opening, silence, a red `ADDON_ACTION_BLOCKED` error naming Tracking Eye, or the panel appearing on its own seconds later.

**18.** Hover the mini-map button and read the tooltip top to bottom. It must show, in order: **Tracking Eye** with the version on the right; **Tracking Menu** with *Left-Click* / *Open*; **Persistent Tracking Ability** with your ability's icon and name beneath it on the right, and *Right-Click* / *Clear Tracking*; **Farm Mode** reading Enabled or Disabled, with *Shift + Left-Click* / *Toggle*; a **Farm Mode Status** block while Farm Mode is on; **Automatic Target Tracking** reading Enabled or Disabled, with *Shift + Right-Click* / *Toggle*; and last **Tracking Eye Options** with *Shift + Middle-Click*. No description may run past two lines. Now do each click. Left-Click opens the **Tracking Menu** beside the button, one row per tracking ability you know, each with its icon, in alphabetical order; pick one and it must be cast, the button's icon must change to it, and reopening the menu must show a check mark on it. Right-Click must stop tracking, put the default map icon back, and make the tooltip read **None Set**; on WoW Forever and Retail, a town service you switched on in Blizzard's own tracking menu must stay on. Shift + Left-Click and Shift + Right-Click must flip Farm Mode and Automatic Target Tracking, updating while you hover and matching their checkboxes in `/te`. Last, tick **Enable Free Placement Mode**: the mini-map button must give way to a standalone icon whose tooltip and clicks match; drag it, `/reload`, and it must come back where you left it; untick the option and the mini-map button must return. Failure is a missing or extra block, a click that does nothing or something else, an ability you don't know in the menu, or both icons showing at once.

**19.** On the gatherer, with Find Herbs and Find Minerals ticked and **Not Mounted** unticked on the **Farm Mode** page (the defaults), mount up and ride in the open world. Tracking must switch every few seconds, alternating between the two, with herb and ore dots trading places on the mini-map, and the button's icon must never dim. With your sound on, those switches must be silent, while an ability you pick from the Tracking Menu yourself must be heard. Set **Cycle Speed** to **Cycle Every 10 Seconds**: the choices must run in order from 2.0 up to 10, and the cycle must slow at once. Ride into a town or inn: the status must read **Paused** in grey with *"In a town or inn."* Zone into a dungeon for *"Inside an instance."* and take a flight path for *"On a flight path."*, with no tracking cast in any of the three. Back in the open world, dismount and walk: the cycle must stop and Find Herbs must come back within a tick or two. Untick all but one ability and ride again: that one must be kept up, and cancelling it from Blizzard's own tracking button must get it recast within a few seconds. Finally `/reload` mid-cycle and check the game's sound settings: **Sound Effects** must still be on. Failure is tracking stuck on one ability while two are ticked, the cycle running on foot, an audible cycle or a silent pick of your own, a dimmed icon, a cast in a town, instance, or flight, or sound effects left off.

**20.** With **Enable Persistent Tracking** ticked and Find Herbs picked, die, release, and run back to your corpse. Once you resurrect, stand still for a few seconds: nothing may be cast. Start walking: Find Herbs must come back within a second or two. Die again and take the spirit healer's resurrection instead: the same. On the Druid with Find Herbs up, shift into Cat or Bear Form and back out, then walk: Find Herbs must be up a second or two later. Untick **Enable Persistent Tracking**, die, resurrect, and walk: tracking must **not** come back. Failure is tracking staying down after a resurrection or a shapeshift, a cast before you move, or a recast with the option off.

**21.** On the Druid, open the Tracking Menu out of Cat Form: **Track Humanoids** must not be listed. Shift into Cat Form and it must appear and cast. On the **Farm Mode** page, tick the Druid group's **Track Humanoids** alongside **Find Herbs** and tick **Not Mounted**: running in Cat Form, the cycle must alternate between the two, and in Travel Form it must keep Find Herbs up alone with no failed cast. Untick Find Herbs and **Include Persistent Tracking Ability** and stay in Travel Form: the status must read *"Druid tracking only cycles in Cat Form."* On Retail, the Druid's Track Beasts must behave exactly like its Track Humanoids here. On the Hunter, with **Mounted** and **Hunter: Aspect of the Cheetah** ticked, run under Aspect of the Cheetah: the cycle must run. Untick the Cheetah condition and it must stop with *"Farm Mode is not set to run under Aspect of the Cheetah."* Tick it again and switch to Aspect of the Pack, whose condition is unticked by default: the cycle must not run. On foot with neither aspect, the status must read *"Not mounted or using Aspect of the Cheetah."* On the Shaman, running in Ghost Wolf must cycle. **Retail has no such aspects, so skip the Hunter part there.** Failure is the Druid's Track Humanoids in the menu out of Cat Form, a failed cast in Travel Form, a cycle under an unticked condition, or Aspect of the Pack counting as Cheetah.

**22.** Open the game's **Key Bindings** and find the **Tracking Eye** section: one binding, **Cycle Farm Mode Ability**. Bind it to a spare key. On the gatherer, switch Farm Mode off and stand still out of combat: each press must switch to the next ticked ability. Press it in combat, and again with a loot window open: nothing may happen. Untick every ability **and** **Include Persistent Tracking Ability**, then press it: chat must print *"Tracking Eye // No tracking abilities are selected for Farm Mode. Pick some under Options > AddOns > Tracking Eye > Farm Mode."* Tick only an ability this character hasn't learned and press it again: *"Tracking Eye // You don't know any of the abilities you have selected to cycle for Farm Mode."*, and with Farm Mode back on, the tooltip's status must read **Paused** with that same sentence. Put your ticks back when you're done. Failure is no Tracking Eye section, a key that does nothing with abilities ticked, a cast mid-fight or under a loot window, or silence with nothing to cycle.

**23.** On the Come & Get It character, trigger a draft, add a word to it, and press Enter. The line must land in chat as one complete sentence with the node, both coordinates, the zone, and your word. **On WoW Forever, send it for real rather than just reading the draft: that client refuses chat lines carrying raid markers, which is why the line is plain.** Trigger another and press `Esc`: the draft must vanish and **nothing may be sent**. Failure is the client refusing the line, a half-rendered line, your edit lost, or anything going out on its own.

**24.** On the Come & Get It character, type `/reload`, then open **Diagnostic Tools**: **Enable Diagnostic Tools** must be **off**, even though you ticked it in step 2, with only the warning paragraph and the toggle showing. Tick it: fourteen sections must appear. Click **Test Event Registration** (every row must read `[PASS]`) and **Test WoW API Endpoints** (no row may read `[FAIL]`; `[n/a]` marks the half of a compatibility pair this client doesn't use). Click **Start Event Log**, spam an ability until red error text has shown a dozen times, trigger one draft, and click **Show Captured Events**. Read the block at the end headed *"Suppressed uncorrelated traffic, biggest first"*: your errors must appear there as one counted row each, in the shape `UI_ERROR_MESSAGE(56, Ability is not ready yet.) x12`, and the gather error behind your draft must **not** be in that block. Failure is the toggle on after the reload, a `[FAIL]` row, no summary block, or the gather error counted in it.

**25.** *Optional, non-English client only.* Read the main page, the Farm Mode page, the Come & Get It page, and the mini-map tooltip in that language, then ride into a town and hover the button. Every label, description, note, and pause reason must render as words in that language, and the welcome line and the **Cycle Speed** choices must carry their numbers in sensible places. Trigger all three Come & Get It drafts: each must read as one complete sentence with the node, both coordinates, and the zone in sensible places; if herbs and veins stay silent while chests work, the profession names in the **Come & Get It Context** report under Diagnostic Tools don't match what that client prints. On koKR or ruRU, trigger a draft on a long-named node in a long-named zone: past 255 bytes, chat must print a warning that the draft is over the chat limit, and the draft must still open in full. Failure is a raw key such as `TARGET_TRACKING_DESCRIPTION` or `FARM_PAUSED_STANDING_STILL` on screen, `nil` or a stray `%s`, chests working while herbs and veins stay silent, or an over-long draft cut short. ruRU is the most useful client to have: its strings run long, so a layout overrun shows there first.

When every step passes on each of Classic Era, Season of Discovery, TBC Anniversary, WoW Forever, MoP Classic, and Retail, manual testing is complete. Proceed to `4 - Pre-Launch Review Prompt.md`.
