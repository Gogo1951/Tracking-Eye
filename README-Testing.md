# Tracking Eye // Manual Test Plan

This is the manual test plan for Tracking Eye, the steps to confirm it works before a release is tagged. For what it does, see [README.md](https://github.com/Gogo1951/Tracking-Eye/blob/main/README.md); for how it works, see [README-Technical.md](https://github.com/Gogo1951/Tracking-Eye/blob/main/README-Technical.md).

## Before you start

**Run the whole list on each flavor in turn: Classic Era, Season of Discovery on the Classic Era client, TBC Anniversary, WoW Forever, MoP Classic, and Retail.** Steps are numbered continuously so you can report "failed on step N."

Gather these once so you aren't caught short mid-run:

- **A gatherer**: a character with **Herbalism and Mining**, on a class with no tracking of its own. It carries most of the plan, since Find Herbs and Find Minerals are the two abilities Farm Mode exists to rotate. A Shaman is ideal, because it also covers Ghost Wolf.
- **A Hunter** that knows Track Beasts, Track Undead, Track Humanoids, and at least two other tracking abilities, plus Aspect of the Cheetah and Aspect of the Pack on its action bar.
- **A Druid with Herbalism**, with Cat Form, Travel Form, and Cat Form's own Track Humanoids.
- **A Shaman with Ghost Wolf and Herbalism or Mining**, if your gatherer isn't one.
- **An angler** that knows Find Fish and one other tracking ability, with a fishing pole in its bags, on every flavor but Classic Era, which has no Find Fish.
- **A Come & Get It character** with neither Herbalism nor Mining that isn't a Rogue. A fresh low-level character is ideal.
- **A spare key** nothing else is bound to.
- **In your bags:** a locked lockbox, such as a Battered or Worn Lockbox. It is a negative test.
- **Out in the world:** herb nodes and ore veins, a locked treasure chest (Battered and Tattered chests along the Wetlands and Hillsbrad coastlines are reliable on the Classic clients), hostile beasts and undead, a critter, a town or inn, a dungeon entrance, a flight path, something to fight, and a mount for the gatherer and the Hunter.
- **Lua errors on screen:** type `/console scriptErrors 1` once, or run BugSack, or a broken step can pass in silence.
- **A non-English client**, only for the optional last step.

No second player is needed: Tracking Eye never sends chat on its own. Unless a step says otherwise, be out of combat and out in the open world. "Trigger a draft" always means: on the Come & Get It character, right-click an herb, vein, or locked chest you can't gather, and wait more than five seconds between attempts. Tracking only comes back on its own while you're moving, so keep walking wherever a step waits for it. Running from an unpackaged working copy, the version reads **Dev**, which is correct.

This plan deliberately skips the long tail: it doesn't re-walk every Farm Mode pause reason or every creature type a hunt can resolve, and it leaves out Hunter: Track Humanoids in Battlegrounds (it needs a battleground), resurrections that land mid-fight (they need a second player), the Profiles page, the Taint Log, and most Diagnostic Tools reports.

## Verify this release's changes

This release gives the Key Bindings section a **Set Key** button, takes every class, ability, and item-type name in the options and the pause reasons from the game itself, fits the mini-map icon properly on WoW Forever and Retail, and rebuilds Diagnostic Tools into four tabs. Run these seven first, on every flavor.

**Key Bindings section**

**1.** Type `/te` and scroll the main page. Below **Free Placement Mode** it must run **Key Bindings**, then **/Commands**, then **Feedback & Support**, with the version line last. Key Bindings must hold one row: **Cycle Farm Mode Ability**, then **Not Bound** in grey, then a **Set Key** button, with *"Jump to the next tracking ability with one key, even with Farm Mode off."* beneath it. Click **Set Key**: the game's own key binding list must open, with a **Tracking Eye** section in it. Bind **Cycle Farm Mode Ability** to your spare key and go back to `/te`: the row must show that key straight away, with no `/reload`. **Each client opens its key binding list a different way, so a pass on one flavor proves nothing about another.** Failure is the section out of place, a click that does nothing, a Lua error, or a chat line telling you to open the game menu yourself instead of the list opening.

**2.** With `/te` still open, pull a mob and, while in combat, click **Set Key**. Chat must print *"Tracking Eye // As a safety precaution, the key binding list cannot be opened during combat."* and the list must stay shut. If the Options window closes by itself when the fight starts, note the flavor and skip this step there. Failure is the list opening, silence, or a red `ADDON_ACTION_BLOCKED` error naming Tracking Eye.

**Names come from the game**

**3.** Open `/te`. Under **Enable Persistent Tracking**, the three options must read exactly **Find Fish when you Equip a Fishing Pole** (absent on Classic Era, which has no Find Fish), **Druid: Track Humanoids when you Shift into Cat Form**, and **Hunter: Track Humanoids in Battlegrounds**; hover the Druid one and its tip must read *"Waits until you leave stealth, and your own pick comes back when you shift out."* On the **Farm Mode** page, the conditions must read **Druid: Travel Forms**, **Hunter: Aspect of the Cheetah**, **Hunter: Aspect of the Pack**, and **Shaman: Ghost Wolf** (Retail has no Hunter rows), and the Druid group's **Track Humanoids** must carry the note *"Only cycles in Cat Form, which counts as Not Mounted."* Hover **Druid: Travel Forms**: the tip must read *"Aquatic Form counts too."* on Classic Era, Season of Discovery, and WoW Forever, and *"Aquatic Form and Flight Form count too."* on TBC Anniversary and MoP Classic; Retail must show no such line, since every form there is called Travel Form. On the **Come & Get It** page, the dropdown must offer **Local (/1)**, **Say**, **Yell**, **Party**, **Guild**. **These names are now read from the game, so a client that names something differently shows it here first.** Failure is a blank where a name belongs (*": Travel Forms"*, or *"when you Equip a"* with nothing after it), a plural such as *Fishing Poles*, a stray `%s`, or a raw key such as `OPTIONS_FARM_CLASS_STATE`.

**4.** On the Hunter, on the **Farm Mode** page, tick **Mounted**, untick **Not Mounted** and both Hunter conditions, and switch Farm Mode on. Run with Aspect of the Cheetah up, hover the mini-map button, and keep the mouse there: **Farm Mode Status** must read **Paused** with *"Farm Mode is not set to run under Aspect of the Cheetah."* Still hovering, switch to Aspect of the Pack from your action bar: within a tick the line must change to *"Farm Mode is not set to run under Aspect of the Pack."* without you moving the mouse. Tick **Hunter: Aspect of the Cheetah**, drop the aspect, and walk: *"Not mounted or using Aspect of the Cheetah."* On the Shaman, with **Shaman: Ghost Wolf** ticked, walk on foot: *"Not mounted or in Ghost Wolf."* On the Druid, tick only the Druid group's **Track Humanoids**, untick **Include Persistent Tracking Ability**, and walk out of Cat Form: *"Druid tracking only cycles in Cat Form."* Put your ticks back when you're done. **Retail has no Hunter conditions, so skip the Hunter half there.** Failure is the old aspect's name staying up after you switch, a blank where the aspect, form, or class belongs, or a stray `%s`.

**Mini-map icon on WoW Forever and Retail**

**5.** On WoW Forever and Retail, look closely at the mini-map button: the ability icon must sit centered inside the round border, clipped to a circle, with no square corners showing. Pick two different abilities from the Tracking Menu, then Right-Click to Clear Tracking: the icon must stay centered and round each time. On every other flavor the button must look exactly as it did last release. Failure is an icon sitting off to one side, square corners poking past the ring, or the icon vanishing after a pick.

**Diagnostic Tools in four tabs**

**6.** Open `/te`, go to **Diagnostic Tools**, and tick **Enable Diagnostic Tools**. Four tabs must appear: **Run Tests**, **Settings**, **Code**, and **Data**. On **Settings**, click **Run All Settings Reports**: the six rows (Player & Spell Context, Farm Mode Context, Come & Get It Context, Saved Variables, Display Context, Other Add-ons) must each turn **Done** in turn. The box below must open with one header line that ends naming this client: `Flavor Vanilla // Data Vanilla` on Classic Era, `Flavor Vanilla (Season of Discovery) // Data Discovery` on Season of Discovery, `Flavor TBC // Data TBC` on TBC Anniversary, `Flavor Camelot // Data Camelot` on WoW Forever, `Flavor Mists // Data Mists` on MoP Classic, and `Flavor Mainline // Data Mainline` on Retail. A `---- Player & Spell Context ----` block and one block per report must follow, with no second header. Run it again and click **Stop** partway: the finished reports must stay in the box, and the row that was cut off must read **Stopped**. On **Code**, click **Run All Code Reports**: the **Event Registration** row must read *all register*. Failure is a missing tab, a report stuck on Running, a wrong flavor or data name, the header repeated in every block, or a Lua error.

**7.** Open the **Data** tab and click **Validate All Data Files**. Two rows must run in turn, **Validate Data: Vanilla/Spells-Vanilla.lua** and **Validate Data: Vanilla/Zones-Vanilla.lua** (each flavor shows its own data folder in place of Vanilla), the Spells row counting its IDs while it works. Once both read **Done**, the Spells row's tally may show only **OK**, plus any **INCOMPLETE**, which is not a failure, and neither row may show **NOT ON CLIENT**, **ERROR**, or **TABLE MISSING**. **Classic Era, TBC Anniversary, and WoW Forever were checked against their own clients for this release; Season of Discovery, MoP Classic, and Retail still run data copied from another flavor, so this step is most likely to fail there.** Failure is any of those three words in a tally: paste the whole box into your bug report, since it names the exact entries to fix.

When steps 1-7 pass on every flavor, this release's changes are verified. Proceed to `4 - Pre-Launch Review Prompt.md`.

## Core checks

**8.** Log in with Tracking Eye enabled. No Lua error may appear, and one line must print in chat: *"Tracking Eye // Version Dev. Settings (including the option to disable this message) can be found under Options > AddOns > Tracking Eye. Enjoying the add-on? Tell a friend about it! (="*, with the release number in place of Dev on a packaged build. Type `/reload`: the UI must come back clean and the same line must print again. At character select, the **AddOns** list must show Tracking Eye once, with its icon, not marked out of date. Failure is any error naming Tracking Eye, a welcome line with `nil` or a stray `%s` in it, or the add-on missing from the list.

**9.** Open the settings three ways in turn: type `/te`, the add-on's only slash command; Shift + Middle-Click the mini-map button; and press `Esc`, choose **Options**, then **AddOns**, and select **Tracking Eye**. Each must show the settings **docked inside the Blizzard Options window**, with Tracking Eye selected in the category list on the left and **Farm Mode**, **Come & Get It**, **Profiles**, and **Diagnostic Tools** beneath it in that order. Failure looks like either nothing happening at all, or a standalone window floating free of the Options frame. **TBC Anniversary is the flavor that historically breaks this, so a tester who runs only Classic Era has not finished.**

**10.** Pull a mob and, while still in combat, type `/te`, then Shift + Middle-Click the mini-map button. Both times chat must print *"Tracking Eye // As a safety precaution, the Options Interface cannot be opened during combat."* and the panel must stay shut. Finish the fight and stand still: the panel must **not** open by itself afterwards. Failure is the panel opening, silence, a red `ADDON_ACTION_BLOCKED` error naming Tracking Eye, or the panel appearing on its own seconds later.

**11.** Hover the mini-map button and read the tooltip top to bottom. It must show, in order: **Tracking Eye** with the version on the right; **Tracking Menu** with *Left-Click* / *Open*; **Persistent Tracking Ability** with your ability's icon and name beneath it on the right, and *Right-Click* / *Clear Tracking*; **Farm Mode** reading Enabled or Disabled, with *Shift + Left-Click* / *Toggle*; a **Farm Mode Status** block while Farm Mode is on; **Automatic Target Tracking** reading Enabled or Disabled, with *Shift + Right-Click* / *Toggle*; and last **Tracking Eye Options** with *Shift + Middle-Click*. No description may run past two lines. Now do each click. Left-Click opens the **Tracking Menu** beside the button, one row per tracking ability you know, each with its icon, in alphabetical order; pick one and it must be cast, the button's icon must change to it, and reopening the menu must show a check mark on it. Right-Click must stop tracking, put the default map icon back, and make the tooltip read **None Set**; on WoW Forever and Retail, a town service you switched on in Blizzard's own tracking menu must stay on. Shift + Left-Click and Shift + Right-Click must flip Farm Mode and Automatic Target Tracking, updating while you hover and matching their checkboxes in `/te`. Only on Classic Era, Season of Discovery included, tick **Use the Default Tracking Button** and click Blizzard's own tracking icon: only the Tracking Eye menu may open. Last, tick **Enable Free Placement Mode**: the mini-map button must give way to a standalone icon whose tooltip and clicks match; drag it, `/reload`, and it must come back where you left it; untick the option and the mini-map button must return. Failure is a missing or extra block, a click that does nothing or something else, an ability you don't know in the menu, two menus at once, or both icons showing at once.

**12.** On the gatherer, with Find Herbs and Find Minerals ticked and **Not Mounted** unticked on the **Farm Mode** page (the defaults), zoom the mini-map all the way in, then mount up and ride in the open world. As the first cast goes out, the mini-map must zoom all the way out, and zooming back in must stick for the rest of the ride. Tracking must switch every few seconds, alternating between the two, with herb and ore dots trading places on the mini-map, and the button's icon must never dim. With your sound on, those switches must be silent, while an ability you pick from the Tracking Menu yourself must be heard. Set **Cycle Speed** to **Cycle Every 10 Seconds**: the choices must run in order from 2.0 up to 10, and the cycle must slow at once. Untick all but one ability and ride again: that one must be kept up, and cancelling it from Blizzard's own tracking button must get it recast within a few seconds. Finally `/reload` mid-cycle and check the game's sound settings: **Sound Effects** must still be on. **WoW Forever and Retail keep the mini-map zoom buttons in a different place, so watch the zoom there.** Failure is tracking stuck on one ability while two are ticked, an audible cycle or a silent pick of your own, a dimmed icon, a zoom that fights you mid-ride, or sound effects left off.

**13.** Still on the gatherer with Farm Mode on, hover the button at each of these and read **Farm Mode Status**: stop your mount (**Paused**, *"Standing still."*, then autorun with Num Lock and it must resume within a tick); select a hostile mob without attacking it (*"Targeting something you can attack."*; a friendly NPC or a corpse must not pause it); let a mob attack you while mounted (*"In combat."*, with no cast until the fight ends); ride into a town or inn (*"In a town or inn."*); zone into a dungeon (*"Inside an instance."*); take a flight path (*"On a flight path."*). Hover an item in your bags while riding: the button's icon must stop changing until you move the mouse away. Back in the open world, dismount and walk: the cycle must stop and Find Herbs must come back within a tick or two. Mount up, let the cycle move on to Find Minerals, and Shift + Left-Click to switch Farm Mode off: Find Herbs must come back. Failure is a cast in any of these held states, the cycle running on foot, or the cycle's last ability left up after Farm Mode goes off.

**14.** With **Enable Persistent Tracking** ticked and Find Herbs picked, die, release, and run back to your corpse. Once you resurrect, stand still for a few seconds: nothing may be cast. Start walking: Find Herbs must come back within a second or two. On the Druid with Find Herbs up, shift into Cat or Bear Form and back out, then walk: Find Herbs must be up a second or two later. On the angler, with **Find Fish when you Equip a Fishing Pole** ticked, pick your other ability, equip the pole, and walk: Find Fish must come up; put the pole away and walk: your own pick must come back. On the Druid, tick **Druid: Track Humanoids when you Shift into Cat Form**, shift into Cat Form and Prowl at once: nothing may be cast until you leave Prowl, then Track Humanoids must come up, and shifting out must bring Find Herbs back. Untick **Enable Persistent Tracking**, die, resurrect, and walk: tracking must **not** come back. Failure is tracking staying down after a resurrection, shapeshift, or pole swap, a cast before you move or while you Prowl, or a recast with the option off.

**15.** On the Druid, open the Tracking Menu out of Cat Form: **Track Humanoids** must not be listed. Shift into Cat Form and it must appear and cast. On the **Farm Mode** page, tick the Druid group's **Track Humanoids** alongside **Find Herbs** and tick **Not Mounted**: running in Cat Form, the cycle must alternate between the two, and in Travel Form it must keep Find Herbs up alone with no failed cast. On Retail, the Druid's Track Beasts must behave exactly like its Track Humanoids here. On the Hunter, with **Mounted** and **Hunter: Aspect of the Cheetah** ticked, run under Aspect of the Cheetah: the cycle must run. Switch to Aspect of the Pack, whose condition is unticked by default: the cycle must not run. On the Shaman, running in Ghost Wolf with its condition ticked must cycle. **Retail has no such aspects, so skip the Hunter part there.** Failure is the Druid's Track Humanoids in the menu out of Cat Form, a failed cast in Travel Form, a cycle under an unticked condition, or Aspect of the Pack counting as Cheetah.

**16.** On the Hunter, pick **Track Humanoids** from the Tracking Menu, switch Farm Mode off and Automatic Target Tracking on, and stay on foot. Target a hostile beast: tracking must switch to **Track Beasts** within a second or two, while the tooltip's **Persistent Tracking Ability** still reads **Track Humanoids**. Target a hostile undead: **Track Undead**. Kill it, loot it, and clear your target: the hunt must stay. Target a friendly NPC, a corpse, a critter, and an enemy player if one is around: nothing may change. Let a mob of a new kind attack you and target it only once the fight has started: nothing may change, during the fight or after it. **On Retail a mechanical switches to Track Mechanicals; everywhere else a mechanical must change nothing.** Failure is no switch, the Persistent Tracking Ability row changing, or a switch from anything but a living enemy creature targeted out of combat.

**17.** Set up as in step 16 and start a hunt before each of these, walking on after each: walk into a town or inn (Track Humanoids must come back within a few seconds); zone into a dungeon (it must come back shortly after the loading screen, and targeting creatures inside must do nothing); `/reload` (it must come back about ten seconds later); Shift + Right-Click the feature off (it must come back); pick an ability from the Tracking Menu (your pick replaces the hunt); and Right-Click to Clear Tracking (tracking clears and stays clear). Then pick Track Humanoids, tick two other Hunter abilities under **Farm Mode Abilities** with **Include Persistent Tracking Ability** ticked, switch Farm Mode on, start a hunt on Track Beasts, and ride: Track Beasts must rotate with the other two in Track Humanoids' place, and dismounting must leave Track Beasts up. Failure is a hunt surviving any of these breaks, your own pick not coming back, or Track Humanoids in the rotation during a hunt.

**18.** On the Come & Get It character, right-click an herb node. Your chat box must open holding a draft in exactly this shape: `Hey Herbalists! Peacebloom at 42, 68 in Elwynn Forest.` Then right-click an ore vein (`Hey Miners!` and the vein's name) and a locked world chest (`Hey Rogues!` and the chest's name). The coordinates must be whole numbers that match your world map within a point or two, and the place must be the zone, not a subzone. On the **Come & Get It** page, pick **Say** under **Default Output** and trigger a draft: the box must open already switched to Say, holding the message alone; set it back to **Local (/1)** and the box must open switched to General. **This step is flavor-sensitive: each client words and numbers its errors differently, so a pass on one flavor proves nothing about another.** Where a flavor has no locked chest you can reach, say so in your report rather than failing the step. Failure is nothing happening, a vein addressed to Herbalists, a draft that sends itself, the wrong channel, `/say` sitting inside the message as words, or `nil` anywhere in the line.

**19.** Each of these must produce **no draft**: right-clicking a node mid-fight (and nothing may appear once the fight ends); right-clicking one inside a dungeon; a second right-click within five seconds of a draft; a right-click while half-typed text sits in your chat box (your text must be left exactly as it was); right-clicking the locked lockbox in your bags; and any node with **Enable Come & Get It** unticked. Failure is a draft in any of these cases.

**20.** On the gatherer, switch Farm Mode off and stand still out of combat, then press the key you bound in step 1: each press must switch to the next ticked ability. Press it in combat, and again with a loot window open: nothing may happen. Untick every ability **and** **Include Persistent Tracking Ability**, then press it: chat must print *"Tracking Eye // No Farm Mode Abilities are ticked. Tick some under Options > AddOns > Tracking Eye > Farm Mode."* Tick only an ability this character hasn't learned and press it again: *"Tracking Eye // You don't know any of your ticked Farm Mode Abilities."*, and with Farm Mode back on, the tooltip's status must read **Paused** with that same sentence. Put your ticks back and unbind the key when you're done. Failure is a key that does nothing with abilities ticked, a cast mid-fight or under a loot window, or silence with nothing to cycle.

**21.** On the Come & Get It character, trigger a draft, add a word to it, and press Enter. The line must land in chat as one complete sentence with the node, both coordinates, the zone, and your word. **On WoW Forever, send it for real rather than just reading the draft: that client refuses chat lines carrying raid markers, which is why the line is plain.** Trigger another and press `Esc`: the draft must vanish and **nothing may be sent**. Failure is the client refusing the line, a half-rendered line, your edit lost, or anything going out on its own.

**22.** On WoW Forever and Retail, where combat hides much of what add-ons can read, fight several mobs back to back for half a minute on each of these: the gatherer with **Not Mounted** ticked and Farm Mode on, hovering the button now and then (the status must read **Paused** with *"In combat."*); the Hunter with Automatic Target Tracking on, targeting new kinds mid-fight (nothing may switch) and swapping aspects mid-fight; and the Druid, shifting forms mid-fight. No Lua error may appear during or after any fight, and once each fight ends and you walk on, the cycle must resume and your tracking must come back. **These two clients are the ones that break here: the others never hide those reads, so a pass on them proves nothing.** Failure is any Lua error, above all one about a secret value, a tooltip that fails to draw, or Farm Mode stuck after the fight.

**23.** On the Come & Get It character, type `/reload`, then open **Diagnostic Tools**: **Enable Diagnostic Tools** must be **off**, even though you ticked it in step 6, with only the warning paragraph and the toggle showing. Tick it, open **Run Tests**, and under **Event Log** click **Start**. Spam an ability until red error text has shown a dozen times, trigger one draft, then click **Stop** and **Show**. Read the block at the end headed *"Suppressed uncorrelated traffic:"*: your errors must appear there as one counted row each, in the shape `UI_ERROR_MESSAGE(56, Ability is not ready yet.) x12`, and the gather error behind your draft must **not** be in that block. Failure is the toggle on after the reload, no summary block, or the gather error counted in it.

**24.** *Optional, non-English client only.* Read the main page, the Farm Mode page, the Come & Get It page, and the mini-map tooltip in that language, then ride into a town and hover the button. Every label, description, note, and pause reason must render as words in that language, the class and ability names inside them included, and the welcome line and the **Cycle Speed** choices must carry their numbers in sensible places. Trigger all three Come & Get It drafts: each must read as one complete sentence with the node, both coordinates, and the zone in sensible places; if herbs and veins stay silent while chests work, the profession names in the **Come & Get It Context** report on the Diagnostic Tools **Settings** tab don't match what that client prints. On koKR or ruRU, trigger a draft on a long-named node in a long-named zone: past 255 bytes, chat must print a warning that the draft is over the chat limit, and the draft must still open in full. Failure is a raw key such as `TARGET_TRACKING_DESCRIPTION` or `FARM_PAUSED_STANDING_STILL` on screen, `nil` or a stray `%s`, a class or ability name left in English (except on an Italian Classic Era, TBC Anniversary, or MoP Classic client, where the game itself names them in English), chests working while herbs and veins stay silent, or an over-long draft cut short. ruRU is the most useful client to have: its strings run long, so a layout overrun shows there first.

When every step passes on each of Classic Era, Season of Discovery, TBC Anniversary, WoW Forever, MoP Classic, and Retail, manual testing is complete. Proceed to `4 - Pre-Launch Review Prompt.md`.
